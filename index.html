About Me

The Story
Behind the
Edit

3+ Years of
Experience

I'm Febin Lawrance — a freelance video editor who believes that great editing is invisible. When it's done right, the viewer doesn't notice the cuts; they just feel the story.

With 3+ years of hands-on experience across YouTube content, brand films, social media reels, and ad creatives, I bring technical precision and creative instinct to every project. My workflow is built on clear communication, fast turnarounds, and a genuine obsession with pacing and rhythm.

Whether it's a 15-second reel or a 30-minute documentary — I approach every frame with the same level of care.

Workflow

How I Work

01

Discovery Call

We discuss your vision, goals, references, and timeline so I understand exactly what you need before touching the timeline.

02

Footage Review

I review all raw clips, audio, and assets — organizing and mapping the structure and flow of the edit.

03

First Cut

The rough assembly takes shape — pacing, structure, and transitions are established for your review.

04

Revisions

Based on your feedback, I refine cuts, polish audio, adjust color, and add finishing touches. 2 rounds included.

05

Final Delivery

Exported in the exact format and resolution you need — ready to publish, launch, or present.
