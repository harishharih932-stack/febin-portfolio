Get to Know Febin

A storyteller with a timeline, not just a tool.

Febin Lawrance is a passionate video editor with an eye for rhythm, emotion, and narrative flow. What started as a fascination with turning raw footage into polished stories has grown into a dedicated craft — one that blends technical precision with creative instinct. Every cut, transition, and grade is made with intention, always serving the story first.

When he's not on the timeline, Febin is studying new editing techniques, exploring color science, or experimenting with motion design — constantly evolving to deliver work that stands out.

How It Works

A clear, structured workflow — from first call to final file.

1

Discovery Call

We start with a conversation. You share your vision, goals, and reference style. I ask the right questions to understand exactly what you need — so there are no surprises later.

2

Footage Review

I go through all your raw material — video clips, audio, graphics — and organize everything. This is where I start mapping out the structure and flow of the edit.

3

First Cut

A rough assembly comes together — the story takes shape, the pacing is set, and transitions are placed. You get a first look to give feedback before anything is finalized.

4

Revisions

Based on your notes, I refine the edit — tightening cuts, polishing audio, adjusting color, and adding finishing touches. Two rounds of revisions are included by default.

5

Final Delivery

Once you're happy, I export in the format and resolution you need — ready to upload, publish, or present. Fast, clean, and exactly what you envisioned.
